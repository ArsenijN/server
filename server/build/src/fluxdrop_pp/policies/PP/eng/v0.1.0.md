## 1. Who we are

FluxDrop (the "Service") is a self-hosted file storage and sharing service run
by one person, ArsenijN (the "operator", "we", "us"), on a server located in
Ukraine. The operator decides how and why your personal data is processed and
is the data controller for it.

**Contact for privacy questions and requests:** privacy@fluxdrop.me
(or via GitHub: https://github.com/ArsenijN)

This policy explains what data FluxDrop handles, why, for how long, who else
can see it, and what rights you have. It applies to account holders and to
people who only open a shared link.

## 2. What we collect and why

### 2.1 Your account

| Data | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Username | Signing in; shown to owners of links you open while signed in (see 2.5) | Contract |
| Nickname | Your display name | Contract |
| Email address | Account verification, password reset, service messages, notifications you turn on | Contract |
| Password | Stored only as a **bcrypt hash**; the password itself is never stored and cannot be recovered | Contract |
| Profile picture (optional) | Shown in your profile and menu. It is re-encoded and resized when you upload it, which removes photo metadata such as camera details and GPS location | Consent (you choose to upload it) |
| Account creation date, admin flag, storage quota | Running the account | Contract |
| Which policy versions you accepted, and when | Proving you agreed to the current terms | Legal obligation / legitimate interest |

### 2.2 Sessions and tokens

When you sign in, we create a session that lasts **7 days**. The database
stores only a hash of the session token; the token itself stays in your
browser. Short-lived tokens are also created for downloads (**1 hour**, to
allow resuming) and ZIP downloads (**about 20–30 minutes**). All of these are
stored as hashes where possible and are deleted after they expire.

### 2.3 Your files

We store the files you upload together with their metadata: names, folder
paths, sizes, modification times and share settings.

To detect damaged files, FluxDrop computes **checksums (CRC-32 and SHA-256)**
of stored files, both during uploads and in a low-priority background process.
Checksums are calculated automatically by the server; the list of checked
files (paths only) is logged like other server logs (see 2.6). **Nobody reviews the
contents of your files**, and checksums are not compared against any outside
list or database.

The operator has technical access to the server's disks. We do not open,
view or analyse your files, except where needed to fix a technical problem you
report, or where required by law (see Section 5).

### 2.4 Uploads in progress

For resumable uploads the server keeps an upload session: file name,
destination, size and progress. Unfinished sessions are deleted after **48
hours** of inactivity.

If someone uploads into a shared folder **without an account** (when the link
owner allows it), a random device token is stored in their browser and with
the upload session so the upload can be resumed. It is not linked to any
identity.

### 2.5 Shared links and access statistics

When you create a share link, we store its settings: the path, expiry,
account requirement, upload permissions, preview and embed options.

**Access statistics are enabled by default for new links**, and the link owner
can turn them off at any time. When statistics are on, each time the link is
opened or a file is downloaded, we record:

- the date and time,
- the type of action (for example "view" or "download"),
- **if the visitor is signed in to FluxDrop: their username.**

The link owner can see this list. Visitors who are not signed in appear only
as anonymous entries. **IP addresses are not stored in share statistics.** The
entries are deleted automatically after **90 days**.

*If you open other people's links while signed in and don't want your username
to appear in their statistics, sign out first or open the link in a private
window.*

Legal basis: legitimate interest of the link owner in knowing how their shared
files are used.

### 2.6 Server logs

The server keeps technical logs to run the Service, investigate errors and
protect against abuse. For each request they record:

- date and time,
- **IP address**,
- the requested URL path (which can contain folder and file names),
- the result (for example success or error code).

Sign-in attempts are logged with the username that was entered. When a new
feature is being rolled out, more detailed debug logs may be enabled
temporarily. They can additionally contain your account ID and operation
details.

Logs are not accessible from the internet, only the operator can read them,
and they are deleted after **90 days**.

Legal basis: legitimate interest (security, abuse prevention, keeping the
Service working).

### 2.7 Security measures based on IP addresses

To prevent abuse, the server counts requests per IP address for rate limiting.
These counters are kept only in memory and expire within minutes. IP addresses
that attack the Service may be added to a block list, which the operator
reviews manually.

### 2.8 Notifications you set up

You can have FluxDrop notify you when uploads finish:

- **Email notifications** are sent to the address you provide.
- **Webhook notifications** send upload details (such as the file name and
  size) to a web address **you** choose. From then on, that service's own
  privacy terms apply to the data it receives.

We store the notification type and target, and an optional signing secret,
until you remove them.

### 2.9 IP Beacon (optional tool)

IP Beacon is a separate, optional tool that you install on your own devices.
Registering a device requires a FluxDrop account, and the device belongs to
that account. For each device we store the label you give it, its **current
public IP address**, the software's user agent and the time it last reported
in. Anyone who has that device's access tokens can look this information up.
A device is deleted automatically after **30 days** without reporting in, and
together with your account.

### 2.10 Data stored in your browser

FluxDrop does **not use cookies**. It uses your browser's local storage for:

- your session token and user ID,
- preferences (language, sorting, dark mode, pop-up behaviour),
- records of interrupted uploads (file name, size, destination) so they can
  be resumed,
- which notices and tips you have already dismissed.

A service worker also caches the FluxDrop application files so the interface
loads faster and works offline. All of this stays on your device, and you can
delete it by signing out or clearing the site data in your browser.

If you allow **desktop notifications**, your browser shows them. We don't
receive any data from this.

### 2.11 Service notices and status page

The public status page shows only aggregated technical data: uptime, server
load and incidents. It contains no personal data. Service-wide notices (for
example planned maintenance) are shown to everyone. Which notices you have
dismissed is stored only in your browser.

### 2.12 Content reports

If you report a shared link or an embedded file, we store your report: the
link or file address, the reason and your message, the date and time, your
username if you are signed in, and an email address if you choose to give
one so we can follow up. To prevent report spam, the IP address a report
comes from is used for rate limiting in the same way as described in 2.7.
The person you report is **not** told who reported them.

Reports are kept for **1 year** after they are closed, so repeated abuse can
be recognised, and longer if they become part of a legal case.

Legal basis: legitimate interest and legal obligation (handling illegal
content).

## 3. Who else receives data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not use advertising,
analytics or tracking services.

Data reaches third parties only in these cases:

- **Email delivery:** verification and notification emails are sent through
  an email (SMTP) provider, which processes your email address and the
  message content to deliver them. The provider is **Resend** (Resend, Inc., USA), using its EU sending region (Ireland). We have turned off open and click tracking, so it does not track whether you open emails or click their links.
- **Webhooks you configure:** the service at the address you choose (see
  2.8).
- **People you share with:** anyone who has your share link can access what
  it points to, within the limits you set.
- **Authorities:** when legally required (see Section 5).

## 4. Where data is stored

All data is stored on the operator's server in **Ukraine**. If you use
FluxDrop from the EU/EEA or elsewhere, your data is sent to and processed in
Ukraine so that we can provide the Service you requested. Ukraine has its own
personal data protection law, and we apply the protections described in this
policy regardless of where you are.

## 5. Legal requests

FluxDrop operates under Ukrainian law. We may disclose account data, files and
logs to Ukrainian or international authorities **only in response to a valid
legal request**. We check every request and disclose only what it requires.
Where the law allows, we will tell you about the request by email before or
after disclosure.

## 6. Security

- Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; session and download tokens are stored as
  hashes.
- All connections use HTTPS (Let's Encrypt certificates).
- The database and logs are not reachable from the internet.
- Access to the server is limited to the operator.

If a data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and
the relevant data protection authority **without undue delay, and where
feasible within 72 hours** of becoming aware of it.

## 7. How long we keep data

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account data | While your account exists |
| Files | Until you delete them |
| Trash bin | 30 days (7 days when server disk space is critically low; a notice is shown in the trash bin) |
| Sessions | 7 days |
| Download / ZIP tokens | 1 hour / about 20–30 minutes |
| Unfinished uploads | 48 hours after the last activity |
| Share access statistics | 90 days |
| Server logs | 90 days |
| Policy acceptance records | While your account exists |
| IP Beacon device records | 30 days after the device last reported in, or until your account is deleted |
| Status page data | 90 days |
| Content reports | 1 year after they are closed |

**After deletion:** deleted files disappear from all access immediately, but
the disk space they used is only overwritten over time, as new data is written
and during server maintenance. Until then, fragments may remain physically on
the disk.

**Account deletion:** when an account is deleted, its account data, sessions,
share links and IP Beacon devices are removed immediately, and its files are
permanently deleted within **30 days**. Files uploaded through the CDN /
CatBox-compatible API are not linked to an account; to have those removed,
contact us with their addresses.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU GDPR and the Law of
Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection"), you have the right to:

- **Access** — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate data. You can change your nickname
  and email yourself in your profile.
- **Erasure** — have your account and data deleted
- **Restriction** — ask us to limit how we use your data
- **Portability** — receive your data in a common, machine-readable format.
  You can download your own files at any time as a ZIP archive.
- **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interest (Sections
  2.5, 2.6 and 2.12)
- **Withdraw consent** — for anything based on consent (such as your profile
  picture), at any time

To make a request, contact us at privacy@fluxdrop.me. We will reply within **one
month**. We may ask you to confirm the request from your account's email
address, so we can be sure it's really you.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In
Ukraine this is the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights
(https://ombudsman.gov.ua). In the EU/EEA it is the authority in your country.

## 9. Children

You must be at least **13 years old** to create an account. If you live in the
EU/EEA and are under 16, a parent or guardian must agree to you using
FluxDrop. We do not knowingly collect data from children below these ages. If
we learn that we have, we will delete the account.

effects on you. Storage quotas adjust automatically to the server's free
space, in the same way for all users (see the Terms of Service).
